# Local demo imagery

These images support the local preview and genuine theme screenshot. `docs/` is excluded from the theme ZIP. The introduction pattern's image is separately bundled at `assets/images/citrus-still-life.webp`.

All four images were created for Wild Lemon with the built-in OpenAI image generation tool on 2026-09-30, then converted to WebP. Original generated PNGs were retained. No stock assets or third-party marks were used. Distributed under GPLv2 or later with this project.

## Prompts

- **Citrus still life:** Natural editorial still life of three imperfect lemons, a leafy branch, and an off-white ceramic bowl on warm ivory linen and pale stone. Mediterranean late-afternoon sidelight, muted olive, tactile premium independent food journal. 3:2, no text, logos, people, or watermark.
- **Garden:** Natural editorial photograph of a sunlit raised herb garden, terracotta pots, pale stone path, and an olive tree. Early Mediterranean light, sage greens. 3:2, no people, text, or logos.
- **Table:** Rustic linen table, ceramic plate of roasted vegetables and herbs, olives, sourdough, and a water glass. Soft window light, muted olive and ivory. 3:2, no people, text, or logos.
- **Notebook:** Open blank cream notebook and pencil on a wooden table, olive-leaf sprig and ceramic tea cup. Soft window light. 3:2, no writing, text, people, or logos.

The photo-style images are illustrative demo content, not a claim to document an actual place or event.
